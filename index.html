⚠️ Wild safety — read this

  • Leave No Trace: only use dead, fallen wood. Never cut living trees in parks.
  • Fires & furnaces: check local rules first. Clear 3 m to bare soil, keep flames knee-high, never leave unattended, drown + stir + feel cold.
  • Water: always filter and boil wild water 1 full minute (3 min above 2,000 m).
  • Stones: never use wet river rocks in fire — trapped steam can make them explode. Use dry granite/sandstone.
  • Kilns & smelting: extreme heat + fumes. Only with an experienced adult, outdoors, upwind, with water and a burn kit nearby.
  • Traps & spears: many places restrict them. Practice the skill, don't rely on it; check fishing/hunting laws.
  • Mud/clay: don't eat or drink from untested clay; wash hands; avoid soil near roads or old mines (lead).
  • Weather: shelter first — 3 hours exposed, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food.
